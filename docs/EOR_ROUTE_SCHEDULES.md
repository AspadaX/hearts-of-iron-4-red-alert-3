# EOR route schedules — revised playability budgets, 19 October 2026

These are calculated sequential focus-day examples, not observed dates or mandatory minimums. Cost units are seven days. Each plan first fields ordinary Tengu/VX technology through the same shared manufacturer route. Economic, theater, production and most decision spending are omitted so their opportunity cost remains visible.

Yoshiro can reaffirm directly after the court: no crisis is required. Tatsu and Shinzo can use a Scheduled Court Review (75 PP, 60 days after the court) to open the succession debate without foreign evidence, defeat or expansion. The examples complete the Crown Prince/Emperor's Shadow while that review runs, overlapping 35 days of its wait; allow about 25 additional days plus event delivery. Revelation and defeat remain alternatives. Choosing Yoshiro while the review runs cancels the debate outcome without refund.

For the illustrated bloodless Shinzo route, support the military in the court debate and accept the establishment's subsequent intervention to build sufficient influence. Negotiated and armed routes remain alternatives; armed consolidation additionally waits for an actual civil-war victory. Program funding and PP shortages can add waits not included below.

## Project opportunity cost

From the shared early-aircraft plan through the institute, nanoconstruction, robotic production, mainframe and a two-focus primary project takes **455 focus-days**, before political/economic/theater detours. The old example took 630. The mainframe now authorizes project selection directly; the intermediate permission and research-fee nodes are removed.

After the mainframe, each primary branch takes 35 program days plus 70 prototype days and 50 PP. The program already grants a lasting practical research, procurement or naval benefit. Executioner then needs 150 PP and 180 industrial preparation days; Giga Fortress needs 200 PP and 240 preparation days. Both reserve eight civilian factories and reduce military output during preparation. Deliberate wartime activation and the existing active/recovery durations remain. Choosing refined Executioner reliability adds a separate 90-day engineering wait. Yuriko missions need 30 preparation days after authorization and share a 180-day cooldown.

Preparation can overlap unrelated focus work. These are source budgets; engine execution, actual first-war dates and investment competitiveness remain unverified. All balance is provisional.
